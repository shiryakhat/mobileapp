## Description

Description (EN):

```text
Shir ya Khat (Heads or Tails) is the leading Persian-language podcast on blockchain, Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Web3 — now as a fast, beautiful app.

Listen to every episode with a synchronized bilingual (Farsi/English) transcript you can read, search, and tap to jump to any moment. A built-in glossary explains key terms inline — tap any highlighted word to see its definition.

Features:
• Full episode library — stream or download for offline
• Synchronized Farsi + English transcripts
• Full-text search across every episode
• Tap-to-define glossary of blockchain terms
• Chapters to jump between topics
• Bookmarks and resume where you left off
• Clean, distraction-free player
• Light & dark themes, full right-to-left Persian interface

From the fundamentals of Bitcoin to Ethereum upgrades, DeFi, scaling, and privacy — learn blockchain in Persian, at your own pace.

Shir ya Khat — flip the coin.
```
